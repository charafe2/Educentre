Everything you need

Built for how centres actually work

Every feature was shaped by conversations with centre directors, teachers, and administrators who were tired of patching together spreadsheets.

Smart Scheduling

Drag-and-drop timetabling that automatically resolves room conflicts, teacher overlaps, and student clashes. What used to take a full day now takes ten minutes.

Attendance Tracking

One tap per student to mark present or absent. Automated SMS alerts reach parents the moment their child doesn't show up. No more phone-call chains.

Payment Management

Automated invoicing, online payment links, and real-time revenue tracking. Know exactly who has paid, who hasn't, and how much is coming next month.

Learning Analytics

Per-student progress dashboards that highlight who is improving and who needs intervention. Insight your teachers will actually act on.

Parent Communication

Announcements, progress reports, and direct messages delivered in seconds. Parents stay informed, and your staff stops fielding repeat calls.

Multi-Branch Support

Manage every location from a single unified dashboard. Compare performance across branches, transfer students, and standardise processes centre-wide.
